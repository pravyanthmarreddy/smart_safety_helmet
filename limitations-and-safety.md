# Limitations and safety

- This is a prototype, not certified PPE or a medical device.
- PIR cannot reliably establish that the helmet is being worn.
- Inactivity is not proof of a fall or medical emergency.
- Candidate-fall logic is heuristic and unvalidated.
- Optical pulse/SpO₂ estimates may be inaccurate and must not be used for diagnosis.
- GNSS may be unavailable indoors or stale.
- Raw LoRa does not inherently guarantee delivery, confidentiality, or authentication.
- Receiver logging is not proof that help has arrived.
- Radio settings and use must comply with local law.
- Never deploy as the sole safety measure or use it to replace established workplace procedures.
